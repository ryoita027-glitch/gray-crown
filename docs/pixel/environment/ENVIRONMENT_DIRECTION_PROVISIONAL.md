## Production

## Character controls
- Karen — VILLAGE RUNNER
- Triton — GROUND ANCHOR

Only the approved committed Karen/Triton PNGs are valid controls.

## Design principles
- Background supports characters rather than competing with them.
- Feet and ground contact remain readable.
- Traversable and blocked areas must be visually distinguishable.
- Avoid excessive pixel noise.
- Building purpose should read from silhouette.
- Doors must read clearly as entrances.
- Furniture must leave usable movement paths.
- Grass must not hide too much of the lower body.
- Background contrast must not overpower protagonist faces, hair, or major color masses.
- nearest-neighbor presentation is required.

## Character relationship
Karen uses bright red/red-brown hair, a light forward rhythm, and larger body reactions.
Triton uses dark heavy hair, a low center of gravity, and horizontal stability.

The prototype must therefore avoid both a Karen-only bright background and a Triton-only dark background. The study seeks a middle contrast range in which both controls remain natural and readable.

## Inspiration boundary
Friendly, readable top-down pixel RPG environments may be studied for principles such as clear terrain, lived-in props, readable buildings and interiors. Existing game maps, buildings, tile layouts or furniture are not copied.
