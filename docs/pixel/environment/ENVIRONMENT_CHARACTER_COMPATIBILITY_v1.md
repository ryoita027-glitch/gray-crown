# Gray Crown — Environment / Character Compatibility v1

## Karen
- Red hair must not dissolve into background accents.
- Feet must remain readable.
- Walk/Run silhouettes must not be overwhelmed by background noise.
- Furniture adjacency must preserve the silhouette.

## Triton
- Dark hair must not disappear into walls or interior shadow.
- Legs and feet must not vanish in grass.
- Low center of gravity must remain distinct from the ground.
- Broad horizontal silhouette must not visually collide with furniture.

## Shared checks
Evaluate Karen/Triton at least once at:
- Full48
- Medium44
- Compact41

Environment v1 does not lock a final character/environment scale standard.
