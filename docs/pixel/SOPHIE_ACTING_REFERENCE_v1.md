Category names / runtime architecture do not change.

## Sophie-specific motion language

### Idle
Small, calm body movement. Hands remain readable.

### Walk
Shorter, softer steps than Karen; less weight than Triton.

### Turn
Compact and tidy. Hair and skirt follow with restrained lag.

### Talk
Hands carry much of the expression.

### Surprise
Readable face and hand reaction without large whole-body jump.

### Approach
Supportive / careful closing of distance, not Karen's eager forward rush.

### Laugh
Warm and visible, but still smaller in body amplitude than Karen.

### Run
Urgent but compact. Hair and layered clothing follow without turning into excessive bounce.

## Identity rule

Sophie motion should read as:

> careful, helpful, practical, attentive.

Not:

> generic healer casting animation.

The hands remain the primary acting focus across categories.
