Check:

- face shape / eye treatment is not a reskin of Karen, Triton or Sophie;
- white hair remains readable without swallowing the face;
- posture reads composed and measured;
- body mass does not copy Karen's runner stance;
- center of gravity does not copy Triton's grounded breadth;
- silhouette does not copy Sophie's softer hand-focused mass;
- 44px Medium remains readable;
- no unresolved symbol or final color decision is smuggled into the design.
