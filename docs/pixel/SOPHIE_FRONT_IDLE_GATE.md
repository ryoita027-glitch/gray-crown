## Review result

The current v1 is useful as a first 48px translation because it already proves that Sophie can occupy a visibly smaller footprint than Triton while preserving the same source-cell architecture.

It also successfully carries several locked identity elements:

- honey / light golden-brown hair;
- cream / off-white dominant body mass;
- visibly smaller silhouette;
- soft rounded body read;
- calm front-facing stance;
- no religious emblem;
- no final weapon;
- no invented canon detail.

However, it is not strong enough to authorize four directions yet.

## Why v1 is REVISE

### 1. Adult read is borderline

At 48px the face/body balance is still close to "small cute healer" shorthand.

Sophie must remain clearly a 23-year-old adult even though she is the smallest hero.

Revision target:

- slightly stronger adult torso / shoulder read;
- keep the soft body;
- avoid making the head feel disproportionately childlike;
- preserve gentle eyes without turning them into an infantile expression.

### 2. Hand-focus is not yet convincing

The current hand/green shapes read too much as horizontal side accents.

STEADY HANDS requires the viewer's attention to land on useful, readable hands rather than on green bars.

Revision target:

- both hands visibly separate from the torso;
- one hand may rest nearer the small treatment storage;
- the other may sit slightly forward / ready to reach;
- green should support the wrists / forearms / tool area, not extend as a decorative horizontal stripe.

### 3. Low braid / bundle is under-read from the front

The front silhouette currently reads mainly as a compact honey-hair mass.

Revision target:

- preserve a single low soft bundle / braid;
- allow a small asymmetrical hint beside or behind one shoulder;
- do not add many loose strands;
- do not imitate Karen's moving loose hair.

### 4. Character-specific posture needs one clearer cue

The stance is calm, but "ready to help" should be more readable.

Revision target:

- narrower, stable stance than Triton;
- torso quiet;
- hands slightly available to another person;
- no heroic chest-forward pose;
- no prayer pose.
