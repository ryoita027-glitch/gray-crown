## Purpose

This is the first exact-source-cell Sophie candidate under Pixel Character Standard v1.

The candidate is intentionally limited to one front-facing Idle pose. Earlier image-generation exploration produced broader presentation boards, but those are not production assets and are not treated as completed four-direction / animation work.

The committed asset is the only current Sophie source candidate for this gate.

## Locked identity translated here

The candidate attempts to preserve:

- 23-year-old adult read despite 158cm height;
- smaller, softer silhouette than Karen and Triton;
- rounded face / gentle eye impression;
- honey to light golden-brown hair;
- low, restrained hair-bundle hint rather than Karen-like loose motion or Triton-like heavy shoulder mass;
- off-white / cream as the dominant clothing mass;
- green restricted toward forearms / hands and a very small treatment-storage accent;
- visible hands rather than hiding them inside the torso;
- no religious emblem;
- no final weapon;
- no invented eye-color canon.

## Intended contrast

Karen:
- forward / light / active / larger motion promise.

Triton:
- broad / planted / horizontal / lower heavier mass.

Sophie:
- smallest spatial footprint;
- softer rounded body;
- calmer stance;
- hands remain readable as the future acting center.
