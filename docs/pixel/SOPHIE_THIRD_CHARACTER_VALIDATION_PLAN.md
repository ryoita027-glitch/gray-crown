# Sophie Third Character Validation Plan

## Status

**Branch:** `pixel-sophie-third-character-validation`  
**Baseline:** `pixel-character-standard-v1-provisional` at `e856b1860f8001964258ac9680cfd04cf5e21eca`  
**Character:** Sophie — STEADY HANDS  
**Production main:** untouched  
**First gate:** one front-facing 48×48 Idle candidate only

## Purpose

This phase tests whether Pixel Character Standard v1 can support a third protagonist whose identity is neither Karen's forward/light motion nor Triton's grounded horizontal weight.

Sophie must prove a new axis:

> **hands as the main acting focus**

The goal is not to produce a generic healer or a smaller Karen.

## Locked identity

Sophie is:

- 23-year-old adult woman;
- 158cm, smallest of the four heroes;
- soft standard body shape;
- rounded oval face;
- slightly drooping / gentle eyes;
- calm expression;
- small movements;
- honey to light golden-brown hair;
- chest-length hair source, gathered into one low loose braid / bundle;
- naturally light skin;
- right-handed;
- raised in an orphanage;
- church / treatment work experience;
- wants to become a sister;
- kind, but extremely stubborn where life is concerned;
- personal color: off-white / white + green;
- not a high-ranking cleric.

## Selected visual identity

**Sophie B — STEADY HANDS**

Official identity sentence:

> 柔らかく小柄だが、誰かを助ける時には身体も手も迷わず相手へ向く23歳女性。安心感の奥に、命のことだけは絶対に譲らない芯が見える。

## Front-facing Idle visual requirements

The candidate must prioritize:

1. small but adult silhouette;
2. softer body mass, not thin-child proportions;
3. rounded face and gentle eyes without making her infantile;
4. honey-colored hair kept as a readable large mass;
5. one low loose braid / bundle whose start sits near the base of the neck;
6. off-white / cream as the dominant body color;
7. green concentrated around forearms / hands / small treatment storage rather than a giant green torso block;
8. hands visible and naturally ready to move toward another person;
9. sleeves visibly practical for treatment work;
10. clean, washable, travel-adjusted Stage A clothing;
11. no religious emblem;
12. no final weapon design;
13. no eye-color canon invention.

## Required contrast

Before color, Sophie must not look like Karen or Triton.

- Karen: forward-ready, more active torso, stronger motion promise.
- Triton: broader shoulders, planted feet, lower/heavier horizontal mass.
- Sophie: smallest footprint, softer rounded body, narrower calm stance, hands kept readable near the front of the body.

Sophie should feel capable and dependable rather than fragile.

## Fail conditions

Reject or revise if she reads as:

- a child;
- a generic cute healer;
- a nun / high-ranking cleric by costume symbol alone;
- Karen with honey hair;
- Triton shrunk down;
- a character whose hands disappear into sleeves/body mass;
- a costume overloaded with medical bottles, crosses, charms, or fantasy-healer iconography;
- a sprite where green spreads so widely that the hand-focus idea is lost.

## First decision gate

Evaluate the single front candidate on:

1. adult 23-year-old read;
2. same-game Chibi language;
3. distinct silhouette before color;
4. hand visibility at 48px source;
5. likely survival at 44px Medium;
6. off-white main mass + hand-focused green;
7. soft body without weakness;
8. hair mass readability;
9. non-generic personality;
10. room for future Reach / Support / Still Anger acting.

## Stop rule

Do **not** proceed to four directions or eight animations until the front-facing Idle direction is visually accepted.

After acceptance:

front accepted  
→ four-direction Idle  
→ Karen + Triton + Sophie matched-framing comparison  
→ eight Sophie-specific animation categories  
→ 48 / 44 / 41 scale comparison  
→ full regression  
→ GO / REVISE / NO-GO
