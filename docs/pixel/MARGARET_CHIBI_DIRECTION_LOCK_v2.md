## Character contrast

Margaret should differ from the existing three through silhouette and acting before color.

- Karen: forward / active / whole-body energy.
- Triton: low / grounded / broad weight.
- Sophie: small / warm / hand-focused support.
- Margaret: upright / measured / restrained / vertical.

Margaret must not become:

- a white-haired Sophie recolor;
- a narrow Karen recolor;
- a severe or frightening "cold mage" face;
- a hair-heavy silhouette that hides the body;
- a costume-detail dump that loses readability at Medium44.

- Idle: minimal, upright, composed.
- Walk: small measured steps.
- Turn: controlled, head/gaze-first feel.
- Talk: restrained hand motion.
- Surprise: readable but not a large jump.
- Approach: deliberate and soft.
- Laugh: warm, smaller amplitude than Karen.
- Run: light and efficient; ponytail sways cleanly without dominating the body.
