# Gray Crown — Player Agency / Interaction Quality Standard v1

**Purpose:** make Story 00–15 enjoyable *to play*, rather than inflate dialogue volume or add unapproved canon.

## Game loop players should perceive
- **Goal and local affordance:** existing objective in HUD + near-front Enter hint + only current mandatory marker. Reading menus must never be required to know *which actual person/object* to approach.
- **Discovery with a payoff:** original extra NPC/treasure/battle flags are visibly tracked as **optional**. A displayed optional counter may NOT gate exiting an area and may NOT award extra unsanctioned loot.
- **Battle judgment:** the player chooses who to attack when multiple foes are alive. The selected foe, HP meter, enemy card and actual damage recipient must agree. A fallen foe cannot be selected. Retarget automatically on death.
- **Team identity:** Karen's guard *really protects another member*; Triton weakens defenses, Sophie conserves HP resources, Margaret can reduce enemy attack. Fights should create at least two viable decisions per turn; prove by actual playtest, not a design document alone.
- **Readability:** show factual skill descriptions and imminent existing boss phase thresholds. No untelegraphed numerical boss buffs, misleading vague abilities or hidden auto-target rule.
- **Immediate presentation:** use original 48px sprites for normal dialogue and battle; preserve exact original story as the authority. New synthetic music is merely a prototype soundscape until real listening evaluation.

## Test-driven acceptance
1. A new player should locate the village messenger and Ash Road mother, orders and patrol without having to consult developer tools.
2. A player can resolve the normal Imperial patrol by attacking the second soldier FIRST; the first soldier's HP remains unchanged from that hit.
3. Karen's Guard message corresponds to *actual* incoming damage interception, and no dead defender can guard.
4. Optional discoveries increment only from real original completed events; progress survives original schema23 saves because the panel is computed, not saved.
5. 390px layout has no horizontal scroll and all touch buttons are comfortably reachable. Verify with real screen, not guessed CSS alone.
6. No Story00–19 alteration, lore canon lock, changed encounter statistics, modified original protagonist sprites, quest flag injection, or new product promises.

**No sales/engagement data exists for this branch.** Success remains conditional on expert art review, authentic browser validation and novice playtests.
