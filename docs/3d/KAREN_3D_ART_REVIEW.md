# Karen 3D Stage A — Player-facing visual review (blockout)

**Verdict: REJECT FOR PRODUCTION, KEEP AS EDITABLE TECHNICAL MATERIAL ONLY.**

The four orthographic SOFTWARE-rendered views of the actual 80-part GLB were inspected. These images are NOT Blender beauty shots and NOT in-game screenshots. Major outstanding visual faults:

1. **Face:** the eyes still read as separate protruding hemispherical lenses. Iris colour is unapproved. Eye contours, eyelids, brows, cheeks and nose need contiguous stylized facial modeling, proper normals, shadow integration and natural expression. The mouth currently reads as a fixed line.
2. **Hair:** crown/back volumes are round masses; bangs are angular segmented tubes; loose strands and low partial tie lack layered flow. The four-direction silhouette is not yet as elegant as the existing pixel design.
3. **Clothing:** replacing red oval vest blobs with individual closed panels improves editability, but panels still read as flat rectangles disconnected from a tailored work vest. Sleeve, belt and pants proportions need sculpt/retopology and cloth silhouette work.
4. **Body:** arm/leg joints and boots currently read as an assembled toy, not a polished JRPG 3D cast member.
5. **Animation:** rigid prototype bone attachment cannot create natural cloth, hair, bending joints, or expressive acting. Ten stored expression-control values are NOT ten acceptable animated performances.
6. **Game scale:** no proven 3D visual comparison at 768x480 or phone size; original sprites may remain superior.

**Required before approval:** a Blender-authored facial retopology pass, smooth anime-shaded face with independently tunable eye/brow/eyelids/nose/mouth, handcrafted multi-layer hair tufts, contour-fit garment meshes, skinning/weight paint, believable idle/walk, and real normal-scale Gray Crown A/B under identical conditions. Preserve Karen B Stage A exactly; do not invent eye colour, kingdom insignia, jewel ornament or permanent sword design.

**Do not mistake technical modularity for character attractiveness.**
